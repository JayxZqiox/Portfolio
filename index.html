Audio AI

ImpactID

Noise-resistant sound classifier

Tells glass, metal and plastic apart from the sound of an impact, even with background noise. It cleans the audio with a bandpass filter and spectral noise subtraction, measures features like spectral centroid and decay rate, then classifies them with a k-NN model written from scratch.

Solo project for Data Communications.

  • Python
  • Signal processing
  • k-NN
